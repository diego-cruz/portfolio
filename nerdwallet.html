Lending Onboarding — NerdWallet Small Business

Fundera by NerdWallet matches self-employed business owners with lending offers through a four-step onboarding flow — Credit Authorization, About You, Business Basics, and Bank Statements. Because the flow asks for sensitive information (SSN, home address, bank statements) before a user has built any trust in the product, drop-off was concentrated right where that information is requested. I led a mixed-methods research study to pinpoint exactly where and why users hesitated, then redesigned the flow and the new Business Hub dashboard around those findings.

Objective

Identify the friction points driving drop-off in the Lending onboarding, focusing on user comfort, trust, and ease around sensitive information requests.

Usability Findings

What we heard from users

Comfort and trust tracked closely with how sensitive the ask felt and how little the page explained why it was needed — the SSN and bank-connection screens were the clear low points.

Positive Concern
Credit & Service AuthComfort · 3.9/5

Opportunities

What the research told us to fix

  • Enhance the visual design across all four screens to read as more credible and less "hacked together."
  • Add clear security and data-handling messaging directly next to the SSN field.
  • Introduce Plaid with a short, plain-language explanation of what it is, who uses it, and how it protects user data.
  • Add a clickable link to the full service agreement on the Credit Authorization screen.
  • Replace technical security language ("256-bit security") with concrete, plain statements non-technical users find credible.
  • Tell users upfront that they'll need their Federal Tax ID, so they can have it ready before starting.
  • Add a short note explaining that information is only used to find suitable lending options — never stored or sold.
  • Surface trust signals (rating, security credentials) prominently, not just buried in the footer.
